The decision this supports

Prioritize investigation before changing suppliers. The Home Office case demonstrates why a concentration point estimate alone is insufficient. A procurement owner still needs legal identities, call-off relationships, actual payments, service criticality and substitution lead times. No stakeholder endorsement or realized savings is claimed.

Data freshness, stated precisely

This is historical analysis of selected 2022–2024 snapshots, retrieved 12 November 2026. It is not a live feed. Automated registry screening has its own observation timestamps in the evidence download. A successful rebuild verifies those pinned sources; it does not mean newer procurement data was acquired.

What would independent review add?

A second reviewer should label a blinded subset, inspect ambiguous identity decisions, reproduce an HHI calculation and complete the dashboard tasks on a narrow screen. The repository includes a reviewer packet, usability task sheet and empty feedback template. No outside review or user testing results have been fabricated.
